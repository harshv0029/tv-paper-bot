## Formula and limits

Base run cost 0.8% round trip. With g = PFgross, n = PFnet, w = net win rate:

    K  = (g - n) / (w + (1-w)*n)
    K' = K * c'/0.8
    n' = (g - w*K') / (1 + (1-w)*K')

- Assumes extra slippage is a flat % of notional on every trade. Real slippage is worse on thin symbols, stop-outs and fast markets, so survivors here can still fail live; failures here fail for sure.
- w (net win rate) approximates the share of cost falling on winners.
- Needs PFgross and win %; entries missing either are listed as not computable (never guessed).
- A screen, not a replay. Empirical stressed replay and a turnover filter are still owed.

Method A = exact algebra from recorded PFgross. Method B = approximation from PFnet and win %, assuming the average losing trade is 3.0% of notional (not recorded in the registry; sensitivity table below). Both are screens, not replays.
